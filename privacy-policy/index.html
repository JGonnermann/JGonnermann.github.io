1. Overview

This website (janagonnermann.com/) is operated by Jana Gonnermann-Müller as a personal, non-commercial academic homepage. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data is collected, how it is used, and your rights under the GDPR.

2. Data Controller

Jana Gonnermann-Müller Email: gonnermann-mueller@zib.de

3. Data Collection and Use

Server Logs

When you access this website, the web server (or hosting provider) may automatically store log files (e.g., IP address, browser type/version, operating system, referrer URL, time of request). These data are processed for technical operation and security and are retained only for as long as necessary for these purposes (retention depends on the hosting setup and security needs).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and stable website operation).

Emails

If you contact me via email, your message and contact data will be stored to process your inquiry. No data are shared with third parties unless required by law.

4. Third-Party Services and Content

This website uses a self-hosted installation of Plausible Analytics to measure website usage. The analytics software is privacy-focused and cookie-free. It does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information; measurements are aggregated and anonymous, with no cross-site tracking and no advertising profiles.

Analytics data are processed on our own server under our control; no data are sent to plausible.io or third-party analytics providers. For the software’s privacy approach, see Plausible’s documentation: https://plausible.io/data-policy.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in privacy-friendly audience measurement). This site does not rely on non-essential cookies for analytics.

This website does not use advertising trackers.

Some features may be enabled in the future that load content from third parties (e.g., an embedded comment system). If such a feature is enabled, your IP address and other technical metadata may be transmitted to the respective provider when the content is loaded.

5. Data Retention

Personal data are stored only as long as necessary to achieve the purposes stated above or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data, to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR), data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Security

All reasonable technical and organizational measures are taken to protect your data. However, Internet transmission cannot be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

8. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available at janagonnermann.com/privacy-policy/.
