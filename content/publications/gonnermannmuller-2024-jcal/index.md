abstract: 'This systematic literature review examines empirical cognitive load studies in augmented reality (AR) learning environments to develop evidence-based design guidelines. The study analyzes 45 empirical studies published between 2010-2023, focusing on cognitive load measurement, design factors, and learning outcomes. Key findings reveal that AR can both reduce and increase cognitive load depending on design implementation. The review identifies critical design principles for managing cognitive load in AR learning applications, including spatial organization, information density, and user guidance strategies.'
